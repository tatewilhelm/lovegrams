# Love Grams Generator

## What are Love Grams?
Love Grams is a fun and interactive way to bring people together by analyzing survey responses and calculating their best matches! It’s often used in schools, clubs, and social events to create personalized match reports based on shared interests, personality traits, or other criteria. It's an easy, fun and effective fundraiser. 

## How do I use the tool?

### Making a Form
First, you need to create a form using a service like Google Forms. *I HIGHLY RECOMMEND GOOGLE FORMS!!!* This tool has been designed for use with Google Forms, and I cannot guarantee that it will work with something like Microsoft Forms.

The form will be how you collect responses from students. It can contain as many or as little questions as you want. However, you need at least 10 questions for good results. The form can contain any questions you like to match others with. It could be general questions about their personality, or questions specific to your school / community (favorite local restaurant, best teacher, etc). 

The tool does require these three questions to work.
1. Name
2. Gender
3. Grade

Name should be a free response in the form. Gender (male, female, and if applicable, non-binary) and grade should be multiple choice. The difference between a match and a friend is matches are NOT the same gender, while friends ARE the same gender.

Most importantly, have fun with these questions! If your questions suck, then no one will fill out the form, and that would be boring. 

An example form is linked [here.](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeGHDzoXvdR3BA2AfVZy10uCzkJM7SiuxhLCFAoqCg1HdPWWw/viewform?usp=dialog)

> AVOID FREE RESPONSE QUESTIONS (except for the responders name of course.)!!! This program checks for exact matches and will probably ont match anyone based off of free response questions, since everyones response is bound to be different.

### Using the Tool
Once you have all of the responders input to your form, you can now generate the Love Grams. 

First, open up your Google Form (i hope you used google forms!) to the Responses page. Then, in the top right, click *Link to Sheets*. Press *Create a New Spreadsheet*, and then it should pop up a spreadsheet full of all of the responses. 

At this point, I would sift through all the responders names. Make sure no one put a dirty name, or that someone submitted the form twice. You can simply delete the row with their response if you so wish.

Once you have finished filtering the responses, click *File* in the top left, then *Download*, then *Comma Seperated Values (.csv)*. YOU MUST CLICK **COMMA SEPERATED VALUES**!

After the file has finished downloading, open up the Love Grams Generator home page. Press *Choose File*, and select the file you just downloaded. 

Then, a list of questions should appear at the bottom. Copy and paste the name, gender, and questions into their respective boxes above.

> Ensure that the name, gender, and question text boxes are *EXACTLY* the same as they appear below. Check that there is not a space before or after the question. If there is, the program may fail to run.

After that, scroll down to all the questions. Begin checking all the questions that you would like to use to compare potential matches. ***Do not*** check the *name*, *gender*, or *grade* questions. If *timestamp* or *email* appears as well, don't check those either. For any reason, if you wish to omit a question from matching, you can leave it unchecked as well.

At this point, scroll to the very bottom and press *Generate Love Grams*. Do not be alarmed if this takes a second, especially if you have a large dataset. If it takes longer than 5 minutes, then it is a bug. Email me at t8wilhelm(at)gmail.com, and I can fix it.

After a generating, a link should appear that says *View PDF*. Click it, and you now have all of your Love Grams! Print them, and sell them as you wish!
